No matter what language you are learning, with just an audio file, “Say it out” will automatically divide it into small sentences. eQuicker24 v1.0

A traditional family mathematical game for training mental arithmetic and quick reaction. There are 52 cards at the beginning, randomly took out 4 cards each time, the first one who achieves the number 24 exactly using only +, -, *, / get 1 point. The game ends when the card is exhausted, and the player who gets more points wins.

Memories on the table
ShowAce v2.0

Without doing anything. ShowAce will loop through your photos/videos one by one on full screen with time and place they were taken. You can easily highlight the best part of each photo to make it look better and more attractive. Of course, you can easily hide the photos and videos you do not want to show too. Also you can draw customized photo frames for each album and add them on top of your photos/videos to make it more special and caring. In addition, ShowAce’s hidden music controller allows you to pause, play, or change the background song easily. Everything you do will not affect your original photos. ShowAce only record what you do and show exactly the same in the future.
